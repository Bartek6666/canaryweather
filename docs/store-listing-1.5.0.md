☀️ Sunly — know your chances of good weather before you travel.

Sunly helps you choose the sunniest place and the best time for your trip to Spain's sunniest regions: the Canary Islands, the Balearic Islands (Mallorca, Menorca, Ibiza) and the Mediterranean coast.

Instead of a simple forecast, Sunly answers the real planning question: "What's my realistic chance of good weather here, in this month?" — using 10 years of historical weather data.

🌞 CHANCE OF SUN
See the probability of a sunny day for any location and month, based on a decade of real measurements. Perfect for planning your holiday weeks or months ahead.

📊 10 YEARS OF HISTORY
Average high and low temperatures, rainy days, wind and rainfall — plus year-by-year temperature charts, so you can see how each year really looked.

🌡️ LIVE WEATHER
Current temperature, conditions, humidity, wind and gusts from official measuring stations.

🌬️ WIND & 🌧️ RAIN DETAILS
Dedicated screens: wind stability, Beaufort scale, rainfall characteristics and regional rankings.

🏜️ CALIMA ALERTS
Detection of Saharan dust (Calima) and relevant weather warnings for the Canary Islands.

🌍 MADE FOR TRAVELLERS
Search any town or resort, or use your location. Available in English, Polish, Spanish and German. Works offline with cached data.

📌 DATA SOURCES
Historical and live weather data comes from AEMET, the Spanish State Meteorological Agency:
https://www.aemet.es/
Supplementary live conditions: WeatherAPI and Open-Meteo. Air quality: WAQI.

⚠️ DISCLAIMER
Sunly is an independent app. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to AEMET or any government entity. Historical statistics describe past weather and are not a forecast of future conditions.
